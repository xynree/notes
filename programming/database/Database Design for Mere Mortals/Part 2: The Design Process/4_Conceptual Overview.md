# 4 - Conceptual Overview

## Importance of Completing the design

- It's necessary to go through entire design process
- Level of structural and data integrity is proportional to how thoroughly design process is followed

## Defining a Mission Statement and objectives

- Identify the purpose of db with a mission statement
- Define objectives that represent general tasks users can perform against data

## Analyzing Current Database

- Legacy or paper based database may be previous
- Review the way organization collects and presents data currently
- Take in account how web based apps work with database
- Conduct interviews with users and management
- Use information from analysis to get an initial list of fields, removing calculated fields for later
- Review iwth users/management

## Creating Data Structures

- Define tables/fields, establish keys and specifications for each field

## Determine and Establish Table Relationships

- Identify relationships, characteristics and establish relationship level integrity
- Establish relationships wetween tables in each relationship with a PK or linking table. Determine degree of participation

## Determining and Defining Business Rules

- ID limitations on aspects of database
- Establish business rules
- Define and implement validation tables
- This can be an ongoing iterative process

## Determining and Defining Views

- ID various ways of working with data and establish views based on how they work with their respective data

## Review Data Integrity

- Ensure table meets criteria of properly designed table/fields
- Review and check field specifications
- Review validity of relationships
- Review business rules
